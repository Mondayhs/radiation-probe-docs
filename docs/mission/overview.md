# Mission Overview | DSRP & CRP

## DSRP — Deep Space Radiation Probe

DSRP was developed for radiation measurements during the HAKUTO-R Mission 2 lunar mission. The scientific instrumentation included a RADFET for accumulated ionizing dose observation and NAND flash for single-event upset (SEU) studies.

The resulting flight experience and anomalous observations motivate several CRP improvements. Readers should consult the original mission documents and datasets for publication-quality statements.

## CRP — Compact Radiation Probe

CRP is a redesigned, CubeSat-oriented radiation payload for COSPAR-1. It continues RADFET and NAND flash measurements while emphasizing maintainable firmware, fault recovery, improved data integrity, and compatibility with the CubeSat's electrical and communication interfaces.
